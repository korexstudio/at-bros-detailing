## Descriptions (verbatim)

**Exterior Detail** (all three sizes share this text; note the stale "$60.00"/"80.00" in the copy)
This is the standard vehicle wash and wax we offer. It includes: Washing wheels, tires, and wheel wells (if applicable). A high alkaline foaming pre-rinse removes the bulk of the dirt, road grime, and other contaminants. Followed with a foamy car shampoo for a safe contact wash. This method of washing reduces the amount of swirls or scratches to zero. Adding on a hydrophobic layer of ceramic sealant to increase beading and provide a sacrificial layer of protection against the suns UV rays as well as from the elements. This will protect the paint if applied monthly. Matte or shiny tire shine applied for a clean look. Air dried with blower on crevices. Hand dried with 1300 GSM twisted loop microfiber towels to reduce scratches when dragging across paint.

**Interior Detail**
This is our standard interior detail (refresh). If you are looking to get every surface cleaned, deep vacuum, as well as air compressed out, then this would be the best option for you! We target areas of high impact i.e: (doors, dash, cup holder, pedals, steering wheel, vents). We also apply a plastic and leather conditioner (if applicable) to restore an OEM-like matte finish which also provides UV protection to prevent cracking. Note: Vehicles with extra size, seats, extra rows, or excessively dirty condition may be subject to an increased fee.

**Seat/Carpet Shampoo**
This is the add on you would want if you are concerned about any stain removal, or if it has been years without getting the seats deep cleaned. We use steam, carpet cleaning shampoo, and extract and neutralize odors and the chemical to revive back the seats and carpeting.

**Clay and Seal**
This package includes the exterior detail. If you've ever washed your car and rub your hand on the paint and feel it is sandpaper-like, this option would be best for you! This is because the air contains specks of iron and other contaminants as you drive around which bonds to your paint and sticks to it which creates this rough texture. Over time these ferrous materials can rust and be unsightly, and cannot be removed except with a mechanical polish or repainting. First we chemically decontaminate the paint with iron remover to remove the bulk of the embedded contaminants. Depending on how long since it's been clayed, we will use a clay bar if it has never been clayed, or a clay towel if it's newer. This will return the paint feeling glassy smooth again, straight back to an OEM-like feeling. After doing this, we spray on a ceramic sealant that is rated to last 3-6 months. It is recommended to have it clayed again after you notice significantly less beading, meaning the sacrificial ceramic sealant is running low.

**Exterior Detail + Interior Detail**
This includes our exterior detail and interior detail. This is the pricing for a standard sedan.

**Drop off Discount**
This amount will be subtracted if dropped off at the service location.

**Maintenance Detail**
This maintenance detail includes an exterior hand wash, tires cleaned, and an interior wipe of the dash and carpets blown out and vacuumed. This is not a deep clean and highly recommended to do monthly to prolong the cars paint and condition of the interior.

**Paint Enhancement**
This service is a level higher than clay bar but below a one step paint correction. If you frequent automatic car washes, your vehicle will have high amounts of swirls and/or scratches. This service will bring clarity, gloss, and remove minor defects bringing the car paint back to acceptable place. Most individuals who are interested likely will want this done.

**Basic Wash**
This service includes a foaming pre rinse, and a hand wash and no paint protection applied. Wheels will not be cleaned and paint will have zero protection. It is highly recommended to have protection applied periodically as it prolongs the life of the paint against UV radiation and maintaining the value of the car.
