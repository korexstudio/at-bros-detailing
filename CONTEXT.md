# AT Bros Detailing

The marketing website for AT Bros Detailing, a mobile and drop-off car detailing business in the San Gabriel Valley (626), California.

## Language

### Selling

**Service**:
A named, priced job the business sells (e.g. Interior Detail, Clay and Seal). Mirrors an item on the Square booking page.
_Avoid_: Package, product, offering

**Full Detail**:
The Service combining an Exterior Detail and an Interior Detail on the same vehicle. Listed on Square as "Exterior Detail + Interior Detail".
_Avoid_: Combo, ext + int, complete detail

**Add-on**:
A Service that is only sold on top of another Service. Currently only Seat/Carpet Shampoo.
_Avoid_: Extra, upsell

**Vehicle Size**:
The tier a customer's vehicle falls into for pricing: Sedan, Mini SUV, or Truck/Sprinter/SUV.
_Avoid_: Vehicle class, car type

**Service Mode**:
Whether the job is done Mobile (the detailer travels to the customer) or Drop-off (the customer brings the vehicle to the detailer). Drop-off earns a fixed discount.
_Avoid_: Location type, delivery method

### Converting

**Quote Request**:
A visitor asking the business to price a job, sent by text or call. Used when the visitor isn't sure which Service they need or Square can't price their vehicle.
_Avoid_: Lead, enquiry, contact form

**Booking**:
An appointment for a specific Service made through the business's Square booking page.
_Avoid_: Appointment, reservation, order

### Showing the work

**Before/After**:
A paired set of photos of the same vehicle, one taken before a job and one after.
_Avoid_: Transformation, comparison

**Finished Car**:
A standalone glamour photo of a completed job.
_Avoid_: Showcase shot, result
